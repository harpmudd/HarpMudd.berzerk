Place your Berzerk romset-built ROM here (copyrighted; not included).
Build it from the bundled berzerk.mra with the MAME berzerk.zip:
    mra berzerk.mra        (writes berzerk.rom)
then keep berzerk.rom in this folder / on your Pocket SD card.
Note: the parent set needs the rc31a revision of rom5.5c (CRC e0fab8f5).
